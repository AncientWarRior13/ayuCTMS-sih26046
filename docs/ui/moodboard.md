# AyuCTMS UI Moodboard & Creative Direction

## Aesthetic: "The Living Ledger"
An instrument-grade clinical trial command center that marries regulatory cryptographic precision with authentic Ayurvedic botanical heritage.

### Color Archetype
- **Ink Mode (Default Dark)**:
  - Deep carbon background: `#0A0F0D`
  - Layered clinical slate: `#101815` (Surface 1), `#16201C` (Surface 2)
  - Fine border hairline: `#22302A`
  - High-legibility crisp text: `#E8EFE9`
  - Neem Teal Brand: `#2FB39A`
  - Antique Brass Accent (decor/hairlines only): `#C8A45C`
- **Parchment Mode (Light)**:
  - Natural medicinal paper background: `#F6F2E9`
  - Crisp linen surface: `#FFFDF7`
  - Muted stone border: `#E4DCCB`
  - Iron Gall ink text: `#14201A`
  - Deep Neem Teal: `#0E6B5C`
  - Burnished Brass: `#9C7B2E`
- **Prakriti Tri-tone**:
  - Vata (Air/Ether): Slate Blue `#7A8FB8`
  - Pitta (Fire/Agni): Ember Terracotta `#E0713A`
  - Kapha (Earth/Water): Deep Moss Green `#6FA36B`
- **Strict Semantic Status**:
  - Ok / Completed: `#2FBF71` (Green)
  - Attention / Warn: `#F2A93B` (Amber)
  - Danger / Overdue / Blocked: `#E5484D` (Sindoor Red)
  - Information: `#4C8DFF` (Blue)

### Typography
- **Display & Large Titles**: Instrument / Fraunces Serif (academic, historic, high authority)
- **Interface Body**: Inter / Geist Sans (neutral, high density, crystal clear)
- **Data Figures, Hashes & Clocks**: JetBrains Mono with tabular numbers (`tnum`) and slashed zeros.
